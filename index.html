Our Services

Services we provide.

01

Harbor & Port Dredging

Keeping ports and harbors accessible by removing accumulated sediment and maintaining berthing depths.

02

Infrastructure Development

Excavating sediment to enable bridges, pipelines and major coastal infrastructure projects.

03

Industrial Applications

Mining, mineral extraction, and desilting of ponds and lagoons for industrial operations.

04

Channel Maintenance

Regular dredging of navigation channels for the safe passage of vessels at any tide.

05

Beach Nourishment

Replenishing eroded coastlines with carefully sourced sand to protect and restore beaches.

06

Flood Control

Dredging rivers, lakes and reservoirs to increase capacity and reduce flooding risk.
